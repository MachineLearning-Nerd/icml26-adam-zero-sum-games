# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Yi Feng, Weiming Ou, and Xiao Wang for developing the ODE view of
Adam in zero-sum games and making the assumptions, stability formulas, and
empirical questions available for independent inspection. The explicit
interaction-dominated and local-error contracts make it possible to build
small, controlled numerical checks while keeping the under-specified GAN route
visible.

This repository is an independent reproduction audit. It does not imply review,
approval, endorsement, or collaboration by the paper’s authors.
